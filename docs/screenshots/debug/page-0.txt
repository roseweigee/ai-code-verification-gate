AI writes the code. Deterministic gates decide. A second AI only advises. A named person approves. Every step is written to a hash-chained audit log. This page runs the real gates, live, in your own workspace.

How this demo is built. Like testing a banknote checker with notes known to be fake: the AI answers in the three tries are a controlled scenario with planted defects. Every verdict, failing test, mutation score, auto-fix and audit entry below is computed live by the gates when you click. No AI key is used here.
